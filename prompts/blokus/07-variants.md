# Variants

The standard game is 4 players on the 20x20 board. These variants adapt it for other player counts.

## Two Players on the 20x20 Board (Each Plays Two Colors)
- Each player controls **two colors** (for example, Player 1 plays Blue and Red, Player 2 plays Yellow and Green).
- Turn order stays Blue, Yellow, Red, Green, so the two players alternate, and each alternates between their own two colors.
- Each color follows all normal placement rules independently. Contact rules apply per color: a Blue piece must corner-touch Blue only; touching the player's other color has no special status.
- Each color has its own set of 21 pieces and its own first move on a corner.
- Scoring: A player's result is the sum of unplaced squares across both of their colors (lowest wins under basic scoring).
- A player's color that cannot move is out, but the player continues with the other color if it still can.

## Three Players on the 20x20 Board
- Each player takes one color. The fourth color is neutral and is shared.
- Common approach: players take turns playing the neutral color's pieces, rotating who plays for it, as a neutral piece placed on any legal position following normal rules. The neutral color has no score and no winner.
- Alternative approach: play with only three colors and leave one corner unused. First moves still must cover a corner, and only three corners will be used.
- Players should agree on which approach to use before the game.

## Blokus Duo (Two Players, 14x14 Board)
- Board: **14 columns by 14 rows (196 squares)**.
- Two players, each with one color and a set of 21 pieces (typically two colors such as Purple and Orange).
- **Starting squares:** instead of a board corner, each player's first piece must cover a designated starting square: (5,5) for one player and (10,10) for the other, counting from the top-left as (1,1).
- All other placement rules are unchanged: later pieces must touch their own pieces at corners only and never by edge, and may touch the opponent freely.
- Passing, end of game, and scoring work the same as in the standard game.
