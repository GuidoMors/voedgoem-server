# Passing and End of Game

## Passing
- A player must place a piece on their turn if they have at least one legal placement.
- Passing when a legal placement exists is not allowed.
- If a player has **no legal placement** for any of their remaining pieces in any orientation, they **pass**.
- A player with no pieces left also passes (they have nothing to play).

## Permanently Out
- Once a player passes because they cannot move, they are **out of the game** for the rest of the game. They take no further turns.
- A player does not re-enter play. This status is permanent.
- Players who are out keep their placed pieces on the board. Their pieces continue to block the others.
- Play continues among the remaining players, in the same relative turn order.

## Voluntary Stopping
- A player may not choose to stop playing while they still have a legal move.

## End of the Game
The game ends when **every player is out**, meaning no player can place another piece. This includes the case where a player has placed all 21 pieces.

When the game ends, scores are counted as described in `06-scoring.md`.

## Winner
The player with the best score wins. See `06-scoring.md` for scoring methods and tiebreaks.
