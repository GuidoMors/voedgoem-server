# Edge Cases and Clarifications

## Placement
- **Own pieces touching by side is always illegal**, even partly. One shared side is enough to make the whole placement illegal.
- **Corner-touching an opponent is not enough.** A piece needs corner contact with the player's own color.
- **A piece may touch an opponent anywhere**, including along full sides.
- **Diagonal contact through a gap:** a corner contact only counts when the two squares are diagonal neighbors. Squares two or more apart do not touch.
- **Pieces cannot overlap** and cannot hang off the edge of the board.
- **Pieces do not need to be adjacent to the board's edges** after the first move.
- **Any orientation is allowed**, including mirror images.
- **A piece that wraps around a placed piece** is fine as long as it only meets own pieces at corners.

## First Move
- The first piece must cover a corner square of the board. It need not be placed in a "home" corner.
- The first piece has no corner-contact requirement.
- If a player's first move is impossible (all corners are covered by other pieces), they pass and are out.
- A player is not required to place a particular piece first. Any of the 21 pieces is allowed.

## Passing
- A player who has a legal move must play. Passing is only for players with no legal move.
- Once a player has passed, they are out for the rest of the game.
- The game does not end just because one player is out. It continues while any player can still move.
- A player who has placed all 21 pieces is finished.

## Piece Usage
- Each piece may be used only once per player.
- A player's pieces cannot be given to or shared with other players (except neutral color rules in the 3-player variant).
- Placed pieces are never removed.

## Scoring
- Only unplaced squares count against a player; the squares of placed pieces do not.
- A player who places every piece scores best.
- The +5 monomino bonus only applies in advanced scoring, and only if the monomino was the final piece placed, and all pieces were placed.

## Common Misunderstandings
- "My pieces must not touch other colors." This is false. Only same-color edge contact is forbidden.
- "My new piece must touch the previous piece I placed." False. It may corner-touch any of your own pieces.
- "I can only start from the corner assigned to my color." False. Any corner that is free may be used.
- "I can pass to wait for a better position." False. Passing is only allowed when no legal move exists, and it removes you from the game.
