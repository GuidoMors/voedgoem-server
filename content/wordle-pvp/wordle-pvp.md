I want to make a new game. It is called Wordle PvP. There are two teams: red and blue. There are no team captains. There is a maximum of 10 players per team.

Each team must solve 10 wordles. Each wordle can be solved in any amount of guesses, but all must be solved within 15 minutes. At the start of the game the wordles are handed evenly to members of each time. When a member of the team makes a worldle guess they get a second or two to see the results of their guess. Then their wordle is added to the bottom of the queue of the next player. Afterwards they are assigned a new wordle from the top of the queue. Each player has their own queue.

If there are less than 10 players on a team, then any of unassigned wordles are added to the start of a player’s queue evenly.

When your team completes a wordle, 1 minute is removed from the timer of the other team.

The content of the wordle queue is not visible to players. Players can only see the wordle they are currently working on, and how many wordles are in their queue.

The game ends when either all wordles have been solved, or the time has run out for both teams.
The winning team is the team that solved the most wordles. In case of a tie in wordles solved, the winning team is the team that solved using the least guesses. In case of a tie in the number of guesses, the winner is the team with the most time left over when they finished guessing their wordles.

The words used for wordle are in the content/wordle-pvp folder.
